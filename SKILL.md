# Hormozi Offers

Convert a product, service, or capability into a differentiated, high-value, profitable offer. Use the named concepts from the methodology as working tools, but paraphrase rather than reproduce book text. Never imply endorsement by Alex Hormozi or guarantee business results.

## Choose the operating mode

Identify the mode from the request. Ask when it changes the work materially.

- **Full build:** Interview the user and complete every stage below. Expect multiple turns when facts are missing.
- **Fast draft:** Make explicit assumptions, create a preliminary offer, and identify what must be validated.
- **Audit:** Score an existing offer, diagnose the bottleneck, and recommend changes in priority order.
- **Enhancement:** Work only on pricing, value, packaging, bonuses, guarantee, scarcity, urgency, or naming.
- **Comparison:** Build or compare multiple offer concepts using the same evidence and scorecard.

- **Explain from the book / Объясни по книге:** Explain a concept, chapter, example, or choice with source pages, reasoning, relevant nuances, and clearly separated implementation advice. Do not require a business interview for a conceptual question.
- **Apply to my business / Примени к моему бизнесу:** Combine relevant book knowledge with the user's facts. Choose full build, enhancement, comparison, or audit according to scope, and explain why the design fits this business.

Start source-based work with [book-map.md](references/book-map.md). For a full build, load each stage's procedure and knowledge when you reach that stage; do not preload the whole library during the first interview. For an audit, examine all commercial dimensions and read the modules needed to support the diagnosis. For a narrow question, read only the matching knowledge/procedure and relevant example.

## Run the full build in order

Do not jump directly to headlines or bonuses. Complete the offer from the market inward.

### Stage 1: Establish the commercial facts

Collect the buyer, painful problem, desired outcome, current alternative, current offer, price, proof, delivery constraints, direct fulfillment cost, capacity, sales motion, and regulatory constraints.

Ask no more than five focused questions per round. Keep an assumption ledger when the user wants progress without complete information.

### Stage 2: Pass the market gate

Score the proposed market on pain, purchasing power, reachability, and market direction. Define the narrowest commercially useful avatar. If the market is structurally weak, explain that an excellent offer may still fail and recommend a better segment before packaging.

Use [market-pricing.md](references/market-pricing.md).

### Stage 3: Escape commodity comparison and set pricing logic

Identify what prospects currently compare and why. Design a category-of-one offer thesis using a specific buyer, outcome, mechanism, promotion, risk reversal, and delivery model. Establish the buyer-value case and the business economics before selecting a final price.

Do not lower price merely to improve conversion. Increase the value-price gap while preserving sustainable margin and truthful delivery.

### Stage 4: Engineer the Value Equation

Map:

1. The buyer's dream outcome.
2. Their perceived likelihood of achieving it.
3. Their perceived delay to first value and final outcome.
4. Their perceived effort, sacrifice, risk, and inconvenience.

Improve the actual experience and its truthful communication. Prioritize faster first value, greater certainty, and lower effort when competitors can copy the headline promise.

Use [value-equation.md](references/value-equation.md).

### Stage 5: Construct the offer

Use divergent thinking rather than settling on the first deliverable.

1. Define the dream outcome as the destination, not the vehicle.
2. Map every meaningful obstacle before, during, and after use.
3. Convert each obstacle into a solution statement.
4. Generate multiple delivery vehicles for every important solution.
5. Trim low-value components and stack the remaining high-value components into one coherent package.

Use [offer-construction.md](references/offer-construction.md). Do not claim to solve an obstacle unless the delivery system actually addresses it.

### Stage 6: Enhance the completed core offer

Enhance only after the core package is viable:

1. Add real scarcity based on quantity or capacity.
2. Add real urgency based on time.
3. Unbundle and name bonuses that resolve objections or the buyer's next problem.
4. Select a guarantee or explicit risk position supported by the economics.
5. Generate and test names with the MAGIC components.

Use [scarcity-urgency.md](references/scarcity-urgency.md), [bonuses.md](references/bonuses.md), [guarantees.md](references/guarantees.md), and [naming.md](references/naming.md).

### Stage 7: Check economics, delivery, trust, and compliance

Verify:

- The business can fulfill the promise at the expected volume and quality.
- Price covers direct costs, acquisition cost, support, refunds or remedies, and adequate gross profit.
- Conditions controlled by the buyer are explicit.
- Claimed proof exists and represents the claim fairly.
- Capacity, deadlines, bonus values, and reasons for the promotion are real.
- Financial, health, insurance, legal, tax, and performance claims comply with applicable rules.
- The guarantee is measurable, administrable, and survivable.

Never fabricate proof, savings, urgency, scarcity, typical results, value anchors, or a reason for an anti-guarantee.

### Stage 8: Deliver and validate

Use [interview-output.md](references/interview-output.md) for the complete deliverable and audit score. Include:

- Market and niche decision.
- Category-of-one offer thesis.
- Value Equation diagnosis.
- Problem-solution and delivery map.
- Core package and price rationale.
- Bonuses, guarantee, scarcity, urgency, and names.
- Economics and fulfillment risks.
- Ready-to-use pitch and offer copy.
- Smallest credible validation test with success and stop criteria.

Use the user's language. Separate facts, assumptions, recommendations, and items requiring legal or financial review.

## Operating rules

- Treat the methodology as a sequence, not a bag of copywriting tricks.
- Distinguish the **core offer**, **promotion**, **pricing/payment model**, and **sales message**.
- Preserve a strong core offer when refreshing creative, copy, name, season, or deadline.
- Prefer one recommended offer over an unranked list of possibilities.
- Explain why each component exists and which buyer obstacle it resolves.
- Optimize customer value and business profit together.
- Distinguish revenue, cash collection, gross profit, contribution and lifetime cohort economics. Do not turn illustrative ratios or the Value Equation into financial forecasts; consult source corrections when using book numbers.
- Keep fulfillment dependencies separate from presentation labels. A component emphasized as a bonus can remain essential and included; do not remove required support while keeping the same primary promise.
- Use `$hormozi-leads` only after a viable offer exists when the user asks for customer acquisition.
